home so schedules, rosters, news, and registration all live in one place —
without juggling separate apps.

## What's coming

- **Rosters and player profiles** with the head coach controlling what's
  visible publicly.
- **Schedules and RSVPs**, with calendar exports and email reminders.
- **Registration forms** for tryouts, camps, clinics, volunteers, and team
  signups — with secure online payment for paid forms.
- **Volunteer hours** auto-tracked, plus PDF certificates for service hour
  letters.
- **News and photos**, members-only when they should be.

Bookmark the site and check back as new sections come online.
